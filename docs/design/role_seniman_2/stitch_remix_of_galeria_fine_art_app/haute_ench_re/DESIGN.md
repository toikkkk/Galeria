## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the serene discipline, intellectual clarity, and curated grandeur of premier international auction salons. It is crafted explicitly for collectors, curators, and fine art enthusiasts navigating original masterworks and high-stakes bidding via mobile touchpoints.

### Core Philosophy
- **Art-Forward Silence:** The UI chrome serves strictly as a museum plinth. Interface elements never distract or compete with the artworks; their sole purpose is to present paintings, provenance, and medium with pristine visual dignity.
- **Editorial Dignity:** Architectural restraint balances warm gallery surfaces with authoritative, classical typography.
- **Quiet Authority:** Premium touches are applied through restrained micro-details, ultra-diffused atmospheric shadows, razor-sharp hairline dividers, and deliberate golden accents rather than saturated decorative embellishments.
- **Localized Sophistication:** All interface microcopy is phrased in elevated, formal Indonesian (e.g., *Jelajahi Karya*, *Lelang Terkini*, *Ajukan Tawaran*, *Koleksi Kurasi*, *Detail Karya*), instilling reverence and institutional trust.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authentic gallery atmosphere using warm off-white museum walls, deep charcoal-black typography, and restrained functional accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1C1C1C`):** Deep near-black. Used for editorial headlines, artwork titles, dominant framing strokes, primary navigation labels, and secondary interactive borders.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C9A227`):** Warm burnished gold. Deliberately preserved for high-value actions: winning bids, primary transaction buttons, curation seals, and auction timer thresholds.
- **Tertiary / Success (`#2E7D5B`):** Refined deep forest green. Denotes verified artwork provenance, active reserve meets, and completed transactions (*Karya Terverifikasi*, *Tawaran Tertinggi*).
- **Destructive / Urgency (`#C0392B`):** Deep editorial crimson. Reserved strictly for closing auction alerts (*Lelang Segera Berakhir*) and live countdown timers under 15 minutes.
- **Background (`#FAF8F5`):** Warm gallery alabaster off-white providing museum-quality negative space without digital starkness.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white used for elevated artwork cards, persistent bottom sheets, and bidding modals.
- **Muted Stone (`#8A8580`):** Neutral gray with warm undertones for medium descriptions, artwork years, dimensions, and secondary metadata.
- **Hairline Divider (`#EAE6DF`):** Subtle museum architectural seams, framing dividers, and low-contrast borders.

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts classical literary French serifs against geometric modern grotesque letterforms, achieving an editorial auction catalogue feel.

### Usage Standards
- **EB Garamond (Serif):** Dedicated exclusively to artwork titles (*"Penangkapan Pangeran Diponegoro"*), curatorial collection titles (*Koleksi Kurasi*), auction hero banners, and section headlines. It gives immediate historic dignity, artistic gravitas, and tactile depth.
- **Manrope (Sans-Serif):** Serves all utilitarian functions including artist bios, provenance descriptions, specs (e.g., *Cat minyak di atas kanvas, 140 × 200 cm*), interactive labels, and numerical bidding states.
- **Financial & Auction Figures:** Numerical values (such as *Rp 1.450.000.000*) always use Manrope Semibold with tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and lining figures for instant cognitive processing on mobile screens.
- **Overlines & Badges:** Micro-labels use uppercase Manrope with tracking set to `0.08em` - `0.12em` to create crisp cataloguing tags.

## Layout & Spacing

Designed precisely for the 393 × 852 px Android physical footprint, the layout system relies on spacious margins and breathing room to evoke an uncrowded gallery floor.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Screen Boundary Margins:** Constant `24px` (`1.5rem`) horizontal padding frames all content streams, ensuring that art reproductions remain unobstructed by edge distortions and hardware chins.
- **Vertical Hierarchy Rhythm:**
  - `32px` to `48px` separation between editorial sections (e.g., separating *Lelang Berlangsung* from *Kurasi Mingguan*).
  - `16px` to `20px` spacing between stacked artwork cards.
  - `8px` to `12px` micro-rhythm within cards separating the artwork reproduction, title, medium line, and bid status.
- **Art Framing Aspect Ratios:** Featured artworks honor standard studio aspect ratios: `4:5` for hero portraits, `1:1` for dual column feeds, and `16:9` for panoramic landscape canvases.
- **Android System Accommodations:** Accommodates top status bar height (`44px` safe margin) and bottom gesture navigation pill inset (`24px` clearance beneath floating actionable trays).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy mirrors physical lighting in an art gallery: diffuse, overhead spotlighting with luminous fall-off instead of sharp digital layers.

### Shadow Profiles
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas Level):** `0 0 0 0 transparent`. Background canvas layer (`#FAF8F5`) with no lift; demarcated exclusively by `#EAE6DF` hairline borders.
- **Level 1 (Artwork Surface Card):** `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.03)`. Used for artwork feed cards and curation items. Creates a soft float above the gallery wall.
- **Level 2 (Active Bidding Sheet & Modal):** `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.04)`. Applied to the drawer where users interact with *Ajukan Tawaran*.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Bar & Toast Alerts):** `0 -4px 20px 0 rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.04)`. Applied to persistent sticky bidding docks at the bottom of the Android viewport.

### Surface Outlines
All elevated surfaces use an inner hairline stroke: `border: 1px solid #EAE6DF` to keep structural edges crisp against daylight or dim viewing environments.

## Shapes

The design system implements disciplined softened corners:

- **Surface Standard:** Base curvature corresponds to soft, sculpted architectural edges.
- **Artwork Cards & Sheets:** Fixed at `16px` (`1rem`) border radius on containers and modal bottom sheets, giving tactile softness without diminishing the rectangular integrity of the paintings.
- **Artwork Media Elements:** Image frames inside cards retain a matching `12px` to `14px` corner curve to sit flush inside container padding.
- **Pill Badges & Status Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for contextual metadata such as *Lot 24*, *Terkini*, and category filters (*Lukisan Modern*, *Realisme*, *Abstrak*).
- **Buttons & Interactive Fields:** Sculpted with a refined `8px` corner radius, creating an intentional, confident silhouette that distinguishes actionable inputs from purely descriptive cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Gold CTA):**
  - Height: `48px` minimum touch target.
  - Background: `#C9A227`, Text: `#FFFFFF`, Font: `Manrope` Semibold `14px`.
  - Border radius: `8px`. No digital drop-shadow. Used for final commitment actions: *Ajukan Tawaran* (Place Bid) and *Beli Sekarang* (Buy Now).
- **Secondary Action (Gallery Slate):**
  - Height: `48px`.
  - Background: `transparent`, Border: `1px solid #1C1C1C`, Text: `#1C1C1C`.
  - Used for non-destructive actions: *Lihat Kondisi Karya*, *Hubungi Konsultan*.
- **Disabled State:**
  - Background: `#EAE6DF`, Text: `#8A8580`, Border: `none`.

### Artwork Cards
- **Structure:** Solid `#FFFFFF` card with `16px` border-radius and `1px solid #EAE6DF`.
- **Image Treatment:** Artwork is placed with edge margins or bleeds with subtle inner shadow to mimic museum matting. Never crop artwork carelessly; maintain natural proportions inside a letterbox container with an off-white background (`#F4EFEA`).
- **Metadata Hierarchy:**
  1. Artist Name (`Manrope`, Semibold, `13px`, `#8A8580`, uppercase tracked).
  2. Artwork Title (`EB Garamond`, Italic/Medium, `18px`, `#1C1C1C`).
  3. Medium & Dimensions (`Manrope`, Regular, `12px`, `#8A8580`).
  4. Divider (`#EAE6DF`, hairline `1px`).
  5. Current Bid / Estimate: Label `Tawaran Saat Ini` (`11px` uppercase), followed by Price (`Manrope`, Semibold, `16px`, `#1C1C1C`).

### Filter Chips & Status Tags
- **Filter Chips:** Height `36px`, pill-shaped (`9999px`), horizontal scroll with `8px` gap.
  - Active: `#1C1C1C` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
  - Inactive: `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #EAE6DF` border and `#1C1C1C` text.
- **Provenance Badges:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`), height `24px`, padding `0 10px`.
  - *Terverifikasi* (Verified): Soft green tint `#EBF5F0` background with `#2E7D5B` text and icon.
  - *Lelang Berakhir Segera* (Closing Soon): Soft red tint `#FDF2F2` background with `#C0392B` text.

### Bidding Bottom Sheet (Android Modal)
- Anchored to bottom of the viewport with top corner radii of `20px`.
- Features an indicator handle (`36px × 4px`, `#EAE6DF`) centered `8px` from top.
- Contains the artwork summary thumbnail, current highest bid counter with real-time heartbeat pulsing indicator, quick-bid increment buttons (+Rp 5jt, +Rp 10jt, +Rp 25jt) with `#FAF8F5` fills, and the persistent `#C9A227` primary submit button.

### Input Fields
- Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EAE6DF`, radius `8px`.
- Text: `Manrope` `15px` `#1C1C1C`.
- Focused state: Border changes to `#1C1C1C` with zero shadow glow.
- Indonesian contextual labels: *Nominal Tawaran (Rp)*, *Catatan Kolektor*.
